## Product
Managing buyouts, partner retirements, and acquisitions of dental, veterinary, and specialist medical clinics. Support full acquisitions, partial ownership buyouts, partner retirement, and succession, including private partner transitions with no public listing or external buyer.

## Operating model
Broker-managed hybrid: approved anonymous public discovery; private onboarding and transaction workspaces; human qualification; explicit confidentiality and disclosure permissions; broker-managed diligence, closing, and handover.

NDA acceptance alone never grants document access. Accepted offers never constitute legal closing. Do not assume dental-only scope, India-only geography, seller-only representation, or transfer of all legal entity equity.

# Competitor notes

Research date: 9 November 2026. These notes record public UX patterns. PracticeBridge does not copy branding, copy, or visual design.

## Henry Schein Dental Practice Transitions

- https://dentalpracticetransitions.henryschein.com/search-listings/
- https://dentalpracticetransitions.henryschein.com/dpt-consultants/
- https://www.henryschein.com/dental/business-solutions/dental-practice-transitions.aspx

Observed patterns: practice-type and location filters, sort controls, listing cards with type, geography, and a short narrative, and a separate buyer registration step before fuller information. The consultant pages explain a confidential sale process for sellers and a qualification process for buyers.

Adopted: category and location filters, a card-plus-detail listing layout, and a clear separation between a public summary and a later inquiry. Not adopted: consultant directories, collection figures, or claims about transaction volume.

## VetValue

- https://vetvalue.pet/
- https://www.vetvalue.pet/connect/

Observed patterns: a seller-first explanation of prepare, market, and close, with different levels of support. The public pages emphasize process rather than an open listing grid.

Adopted: a short, explicit listing workflow and a confidentiality section. Not adopted: fee comparisons, transaction totals, or advisory-service packaging.

## The Practice Market

- https://thepracticemarket.com/

Observed patterns: separate buyer and seller entry points, profession categories, and filters such as state, city, and type. Buyers can browse without an account, while each listing still belongs to a broker.

Adopted: category entry points and free browsing of approved listings. Not adopted: broker aggregation, ranking claims, or live market counts.

## SMERGERS

- https://www.smergers.com/veterinary-clinic-investment-opportunities/s726t5b/

Observed patterns: cards that show location, a short description, and the transaction shape, including a partial stake and an asking figure.

Adopted: transaction type and optional ownership percentage on the card and detail page. Not adopted: public email and phone buttons on a listing.

## BizBuySell

BizBuySell’s own listing grid was not opened in this session. A public article, https://www.vetfirmsales.com/how-much-is-a-veterinary-practice-worth, cited BizBuySell veterinary listing statistics as of 27 October 2026. No BizBuySell layout was copied. PracticeBridge does not publish revenue multiples or sold-price benchmarks in this milestone.

## Design choices that came from the review

- Show city and region, not a street address.
- Require approval before a listing is searchable.
- Use an inquiry instead of printing the owner’s email on the listing.
- Keep the first screen limited to the action a buyer or seller can actually take.
- Do not invent testimonials, partner logos, or completed-transaction counts.
